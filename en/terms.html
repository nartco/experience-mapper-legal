What the app is for

Ipsodex is a personal journal to observe what you live and feel. It is not a medical device: it makes no diagnosis and doesn’t replace a health professional’s advice.

What the app doesn’t do

The app encourages taking no product. For a substance, a medication or a supplement (alcohol included), it never offers to plan, dose or re-take: no suggested amount, no “Redo”, no intake reminder. A name that looks like a substance or a medication is recognised and filed as such.

The app gives no dose and checks no mixture or interaction.

The measurement games describe a difference from your reference; they never say whether you can drive or whether you are safe.

Statistics describe your own notes side by side; they never say what a product does.

The app does not monitor your state and alerts no one, even if you note that things are going badly. When you note it, it only shows you where to find help. Reminders may be delayed or not arrive: never rely on them to take a treatment or for your safety.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number (EU: 112; US: 911, crisis line 988, Poison Control 1-800-222-1222; UK: 999, Samaritans 116 123). In the app: “Need help?”.

Legal framework

Using illegal drugs is against the law in France and many other countries, and carries risks. Noting an intake doesn’t make it safer.

What you share (images, exports) is your responsibility: making the use of an illegal product public can be punished.
