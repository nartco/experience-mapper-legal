What the app is for

Experience Mapper is a personal journal to observe what you live and feel. It is not a medical device: it makes no diagnosis and doesn’t replace a health professional’s advice.

What the app doesn’t do

The app encourages taking no product and never offers to plan, dose or re-take any substance or medication.

The measurement games describe a difference from your reference; they never say whether you can drive or whether you are safe.

Statistics describe your own notes side by side; they never say what a product does.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Legal framework

Using illegal drugs is against the law in France and many other countries, and carries risks. Noting an intake doesn’t make it safer.

What you share (images, exports) is your responsibility: making the use of an illegal product public can be punished.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 or over, which you confirm at first launch.

Researcher mode (subscription)

The journal stays free. Researcher mode is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription, with a free trial, or as a one-time purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store account settings.

Apple’s standard terms of use (EULA) also apply.

Responsibility

You alone remain responsible for your choices and actions. Results (reports, trends, probabilities) are indications about your own notes, not proof or advice.
