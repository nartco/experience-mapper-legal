In short

Ipsodex works without an account. Everything you record stays on your iPhone, in an encrypted database.

We never see, sell or share your journal.

What stays on your phone

Your experiences, check-ins, sensations, notes, elements, hypotheses, research, game results, pulse, voice memos and photos are stored only on your device.

Voice memos and dictation are transcribed on the phone, offline: your voice is not sent.

Photos are copied into the app, without their location data, and don’t appear in your camera roll.

The journal, its photos and voice memos are excluded from your iPhone’s iCloud backup: they never leave the device. To keep them when you change iPhone, use “Export everything” (Settings › Data); you choose where to store that file.

Anyone who unlocks your iPhone can open the app.

Apple Health

If you turn it on in Researcher mode, the app reads your sleep and your pulse (Apple Watch) to compare them with your experiences. It writes nothing to Health and this data never leaves the phone.

What leaves, only if you decide

Exports (backup file, table, PDF article) and shared images go wherever you send them. Substance and medication names are hidden by default in images.

Microphone and photos: used only when you dictate, record a memo or add a photo.

Anonymous statistics (optional)

Only if you said yes: usage events are sent to Aptabase, with no identifier that follows you, no stored IP address and none of your journal content.

Full list: introduction completed; your choices at “What do you want to understand?” (the broad families ticked, no detail); experiences started (live or after the fact), ended (rounded duration, number of check-ins by range, quick or detailed check-in) and deleted; hypotheses created or closed; shares (format); milestones reached (5, 10, 25 experiences); subscription screen seen (from which screen) and plan chosen; app and system version, language.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings.

Subscription

Payment is handled by Apple. To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat (United States), which receives an anonymous purchase identifier, your purchase history, your App Store country, technical device information (model, system version) and the connection IP address; never your journal content. This transfer outside the EU is covered by the safeguards provided by the GDPR.

Legal bases and rights

Statistics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Subscription: performance of the purchase contract.

Your journal only exists on your device: you access, export or erase it yourself. For subscription or statistics data, write to us to exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection).

You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Your data, your choice

Settings → Data: export your whole journal to a file, import it, or erase everything.

Deleting the app permanently erases everything it contains.
